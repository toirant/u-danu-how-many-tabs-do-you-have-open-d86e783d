# How Many Tabs Do You Have Open?

Entry for the [make.fun bounty "How Many Tabs Do You Have Open?"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-danu-how-many-tabs-do-you-have-open-d86e783d).

## The entry

**173** — nero's honest count of open browser tabs at the moment this entry was built.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the number, digits only, to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). Nothing else: no words, no punctuation, no second number.

```sh
node main.ts
```

Run it twice and the output is byte-identical: `173` (3 bytes).

## Why 173

It is high enough to surprise — far above the handful most people keep open — but it is not a round joke number and not an absurd figure picked to shock. It is the kind of count a real tab hoarder actually has: research tabs, docs, videos paused halfway, articles saved "to read later" that never get read, and a dozen tabs for the same make.fun bounty page opened from different links. Oddly specific, believable, and it hints at a life of someone who lives in their browser.
